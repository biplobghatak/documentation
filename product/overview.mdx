Spyro AI is your all-in-one tool for getting found online, both on **Google** and
inside **AI engines** like ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Gemini, Claude and Perplexity.

You don't need to be technical to use it. Spyro AI checks your website, tracks your
rankings, tells you whether AI engines mention your brand, and writes and
publishes content for you. This guide explains every feature in plain language,
step by step.

## How Spyro AI is organized

A quick bit of vocabulary that makes everything else easier:

- A **workspace** is one website you want to grow. Everything for that site (audits,
  rankings, content and settings) belongs to its workspace. If you manage more than one
  website, create a workspace for each and switch between them at the top of the screen.
- Your **organization** holds your workspaces, your plan, your credits and your team.

The sidebar groups the tools the same way this guide does:

## How credits work

Paid actions, like writing articles, checking AI answers, running competitor research
and viewing keyword analysis, draw from your organization's monthly credit pool:
4,000 credits a month on Starter, 15,000 on Pro and 80,000 on Agency, shared across
all your workspaces. Site audits are free. See
[Plans & billing](/product/billing-and-usage) for the full list of credit costs.
