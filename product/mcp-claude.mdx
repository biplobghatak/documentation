Use Spyro AI inside Claude on the web and in Claude Desktop. Claude connects through a **custom connector** and signs in with your Spyro AI account, so you do not need a key.

## Before you start

* You are an admin of a Spyro AI workspace on a plan that includes MCP. See [MCP server overview](/product/mcp-overview).
* Your Claude plan allows custom connectors. Availability and limits depend on your Claude plan, so check Claude's settings if you do not see the option.

## Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open connectors">
    In Claude, open **Customize → Connectors**, click **+ Add**, then **Add custom connector**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the server">
    Name it **Spyro AI** and paste the address:

    ```text
    https://mcp.spyro.app/mcp
    ```

    Leave the advanced OAuth client fields empty. Claude registers itself.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Click **Add**, then **Connect**. Sign in to Spyro AI, tick the workspaces Claude may use, choose **Read your data** or **Read and take actions**, and click **Allow**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn it on in a chat">
    Start a chat and make sure the Spyro AI connector is enabled from the tools or connectors menu for that chat.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **Team and Enterprise plans:** an owner first adds the connector in **Organization settings → Connectors** by choosing **Add → Custom → Web** and pasting the address. Members then open **Customize → Connectors** and click **Connect** to sign in themselves.
</Note>

## Check that it works

Ask: *"Use Spyro AI to give me an overview of my workspace."* Claude should show it is calling a Spyro AI tool and answer from your data. The first time a tool runs, Claude may ask you to allow it.

## Try these prompts

* *"What are my 5 most important SEO issues? Explain each in plain English."*
* *"Which of my keywords moved up or down this week?"*
* *"Where do AI engines cite my competitors instead of me?"*
* *"Recommend 5 blog ideas and add them to my calendar."* (needs **Read and take actions**)

## Tips

* Choose **Read your data** unless you want Claude to make changes in Spyro AI.
* If you connect several workspaces, Claude first calls `listWorkspaces`. Name the site in your prompt so it picks the right one.
* Claude asks before using a tool. Every change also waits for you to confirm it in Spyro AI: open the link Claude shows, check the summary and click **Confirm**, then ask Claude to continue.
* To disconnect, remove the connector in Claude, and revoke the app in **Settings → MCP → Connected apps** in Spyro AI.

## If it fails

* **Cannot connect or sign in loops:** remove the connector and add it again. Make sure the address has no extra characters.
* **Workspace is greyed out on the approval screen:** you must be an admin, and the plan and subscription must be active.
* Other errors: see [Troubleshooting](/product/mcp-troubleshooting).

Claude's own guide: [Custom connectors](https://claude.com/docs/connectors/custom).
