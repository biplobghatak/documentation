The **AI agent** is a chat assistant that does your SEO research for you. Ask a question in plain English, and Spyro AI works out the answer from your real data (your website, your audit, your rankings, your Search Console data, your AI visibility results and live search results). There are no filters or settings to fiddle with: you just ask.

## Why use it

- **Get answers fast.** Skip the spreadsheets and tools: ask a question and get a clear answer.
- **Grounded in your data.** Spyro AI uses your actual website and results, not guesses.
- **One place for everything.** Research keywords, spot opportunities and plan your calendar without leaving the chat.

## What you can ask

Some examples to get you started:

- "Find me ten easy keywords about _cold brew coffee_."
- "What topics do my competitors cover that I don't?"
- "Which of my pages are close to ranking on page one?"
- "How is my website doing in Google Search Console this month?"
- "Suggest blog topics for next month and add them to my calendar."
- "Why aren't AI engines mentioning my brand?"

## How to use it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the agent">
    Choose the **Agent** tab at the top of the sidebar, next to **Dashboard**. Spyro AI suggests a few questions based on your website to get you started.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask your question">
    Type a question in plain English, or choose one of the suggestions. Press Enter to send, or Shift+Enter for a new line.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the answer">
    Spyro AI shows its work as it researches, then gives you a clear answer. Some results appear as cards, such as blog ideas or a content plan, that you can act on directly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve changes">
    Before the agent changes anything for you, such as scheduling, rescheduling or editing calendar ideas, or sending an idea to the writer, it shows an approval card. Choose **Approve & Execute** to go ahead, or **Decline**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give feedback">
    Use **Good response** or **Bad response** on any answer to tell Spyro AI what was helpful. You can also copy an answer or regenerate it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Slash commands

Type `/` in the chat box to pick a focused task:

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/audit` | **Page Audit**: technical health, content quality and how a page compares with competitors |
| `/gap` | **Content Gap**: missing subtopics, entities and queries competitors rank for |
| `/snippet` | **Featured Snippets**: answer blocks structured for AI Overviews and position 0 |
| `/eeat` | **E-E-A-T Review**: author credibility, citations, first-hand experience and trust |
| `/keywords` | **Keyword Research**: search queries by intent, volume and difficulty |
| `/clusters` | **Topic Clusters**: pillar pages and supporting articles, with internal linking |
| `/brief` | **Content Brief**: a structured article brief with headings, entities and scope |
| `/decay` | **Content Decay**: pages losing search impressions or clicks over time |
| `/interview` | **Expert Q&A**: questions to capture first-hand insights from an expert |
| `/citation` | **Citations & PR**: linkable angles, media hooks and brand-mention opportunities |
| `/anti-slop` | **Editorial Polish**: removes robotic phrasing, filler and clichés |

## What it costs

Each answer from the agent costs a flat **10 credits**, however much research it does (web searches, keyword and SERP lookups are included). Sending an idea to the writer costs **250 credits**, the price of one article, and the agent always asks for your approval first. See [Plans & billing](/product/billing-and-usage).

## What to expect

Spyro AI answers conversationally, and you can keep the conversation going with follow-up questions. Your past chats are listed under **Previous chats**, so you can pick up where you left off; choose **New chat** to start fresh.

## Tips

- Be specific: "ten easy keywords about _cold brew coffee_ for beginners" beats "give me keywords."
- Ask follow-ups to refine an answer instead of starting over.
- When the agent suggests good topics, add them to your [content calendar](/product/calendar) so they're ready to write.

## Use it outside Spyro AI

The same tools are available in Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Cursor and other AI apps through the [MCP server](/product/mcp-overview).
