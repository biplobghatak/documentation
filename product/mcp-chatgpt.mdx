ChatGPT can use Spyro AI as a custom MCP server. It connects through sign-in, so you do not need a key.

## Before you start

* You are an admin of a Spyro AI workspace on a plan that includes MCP.
* Your ChatGPT plan and workspace allow custom MCP servers. This depends on your plan, and on Business, Enterprise and Education workspaces an admin may need to turn it on first.
* The server address must be reachable from the internet. `https://mcp.spyro.app/mcp` is.

## Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the plugin settings">
    Go to **chatgpt.com/plugins**, click the plus button, then **Add custom MCP server**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the server">
    Name it **Spyro AI**. Paste the address under **Connection** and choose **OAuth**:

    ```text
    https://mcp.spyro.app/mcp
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept the notice">
    Read the risk notice, click **I understand and want to continue**, then **Create as a plugin**. Install the plugin from your plugins list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in to Spyro AI">
    Sign in, tick the workspaces ChatGPT may use, choose the access level and click **Allow**. You return to ChatGPT.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  ChatGPT's menu names change often. If you do not see **plugins**, look in **Settings** for **Connectors** or **Apps**, and for a **developer mode** switch.
</Note>

## Check that it works

Start a new chat, enable Spyro AI from the tools menu, and ask: *"Use Spyro AI to show my top 5 SEO issues."*

## Try these prompts

* *"Using Spyro AI, which pages are close to ranking on page one in Google?"*
* *"Where do ChatGPT and Perplexity cite my competitors instead of me?"*
* *"Plan 8 blog ideas for next month based on my content gaps."*

## Tips

* Choose **Read your data** if you only want answers. ChatGPT can show the action tools only when you allow actions.
* Every change waits for you to confirm it in Spyro AI. Open the link ChatGPT shows, check the summary and click **Confirm**, then ask ChatGPT to continue.
* Mention your site in the prompt if you connected more than one workspace.
* To disconnect, remove the plugin in ChatGPT and revoke it in **Settings → MCP → Connected apps** in Spyro AI.

## If it fails

* **No option to add a custom server:** your plan or workspace does not allow it. Ask your ChatGPT admin.
* **Sign-in fails:** remove the plugin and add it again, then retry.
* More: [Troubleshooting](/product/mcp-troubleshooting).
