The dashboard is your home base for one website (workspace). It answers one question, _how is my site doing?_, across search health and AI visibility. Open it any time from **Dashboard** at the top of the sidebar.

## Why use it

Instead of opening every tool to check on things, the dashboard pulls the highlights into one screen. A quick look tells you whether your scores are rising, how often AI engines mention you, and which competitors they recommend instead. Use it as your starting point each time you log in.

## What you'll see

### Performance overview

Four cards across the top:

| Card | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **SEO health** | Your site audit's SEO score out of 100, with the change since your last audit |
| **GEO readiness** | How ready your site is to be understood and cited by AI engines, out of 100, with the change since your last audit |
| **AI citation freq** | How often AI engines mention your brand across your tracked prompts, as a percentage, plus how many prompts you track |
| **Domain Rating** | Your site's authority score from Ahrefs, refreshed weekly |

Learn how the two audit scores work in [Understand your reports](/product/understanding-reports).

### AI visibility

Once your tracked prompts have been checked, four more cards appear:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Visibility" icon="chart-line">
    A weekly chart of the share of AI answers that mention each brand, you and your competitors, with your average. Choose **Details** to open [AI visibility](/product/ai-visibility).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Brands" icon="ranking-star">
    The brands with the highest AI visibility for your prompts, with your brand marked **You**. It shows each brand's visibility, average position in answers and, for your brand, sentiment.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Domain types" icon="chart-pie">
    What kind of sites the AI engines cite when answering your prompts.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Top sources" icon="link">
    The sources referenced most often across your tracked prompts in the last 30 days.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

If you haven't tracked any prompts yet, this area shows **No AI visibility data yet** instead. Add prompts on the [Prompts](/product/ai-visibility/prompts) page to fill it.

## What to expect on your first login

A brand-new workspace starts mostly empty, and that's normal. Scores show a dash (`-`) until the first results arrive. Everything fills in on its own as Spyro AI finishes its work:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Your first audit completes">
    **SEO health** and **GEO readiness** appear.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your domain authority is checked">
    **Domain Rating** appears.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your prompts are checked">
    **AI citation freq** and the AI visibility cards fill in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Right after setup you may also see a setup banner, setup notes and a content-plan progress bar while Spyro AI looks over your site. They go away once the work is done.
</Tip>
