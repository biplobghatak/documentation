Spyro uses a simple credit-based model: every plan includes a monthly credit pool that powers paid actions like writing articles and running competitor research. This page explains the plans, how credits work, and how to manage billing.

## Start with a free trial

New accounts begin with a **3-day free trial** that includes **500 credits** and a quota of **3 articles** - enough to take the writer and other paid features for a spin. When the trial ends, you'll choose a plan to keep going.

<Note>
  Unused trial credits are cleared when you subscribe to a paid plan.
</Note>

## Credits - how they work

Every plan includes one **org-wide credit pool** shared across all your workspaces. Paid actions like writing articles, running competitor scans, and viewing keyword analysis draw from this pool.

| Plan | Credits per month | Workspaces |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pro | **15,000** | up to 10 |
| Agency | **80,000** | unlimited |

Credits reset monthly on your billing date. Unused trial credits are cleared when you subscribe.

### Credit costs at a glance

### What you also get

Both plans include:

- Keywords you can track: up to **500** per workspace
- Tracking prompts: **50** per workspace (Pro) or **250** per workspace (Agency)
- Prompt check frequency: **daily** or **weekly**
- AI assistants tracked: **All of them** - Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity
- Connected tools: **5** per workspace
- Team members: **Unlimited**
- Site audits: **Free** (no credits)

## Where to see your credit balance

You can check your credits in two places:

- **Sidebar**: The bottom of the sidebar shows your credits used / monthly limit. Hover the meter to see that the pool is shared across all your workspaces.
- **Billing page**: Go to **Settings → Plan & billing** to see credits used this cycle and your next reset date.

<Note>
  During your trial, the sidebar shows your 500-credit trial allowance instead of the monthly plan figure.
</Note>

## Running out of credits

If a paid action would exceed your remaining credits, Spyro blocks it and shows how many credits you need. You can upgrade your plan or wait for your monthly reset. There is no self-serve credit top-up; for higher volumes, choose a higher plan or contact sales for a Custom plan.

## Upgrade or change your plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Plan & billing">
    Go to your account settings and open **Plan & billing**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a plan">
    Choose Pro or Agency, monthly or yearly. Spyro opens a secure checkout for you to pay.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change it any time">
    Already subscribed? Adjust your plan or billing cycle and Spyro updates it on the spot.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manage your billing">
    Use **Manage billing** to update your card, download invoices, and review your subscription.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Billing covers your whole organisation, not a single workspace. Only org owners and admins can change the plan or billing details. Other team members will see a message to contact the org owner.
</Note>
