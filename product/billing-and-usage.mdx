Spyro AI uses a credit-based model. Every plan includes a monthly credit pool that powers paid actions such as writing articles, checking AI answers and running competitor scans. Billing belongs to your **organization**, so one plan and one credit pool cover all of its workspaces.

## Start with a free trial

New accounts start with a **3-day free trial**. You choose a plan at checkout and enter your card, but you aren't charged until the trial ends. You can cancel at any time before then.

How much you can use during the trial depends on whether the subscription is still active:

| Trial state | Credits | Articles | Keyword research searches |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Active trial (will become a paid plan) | 500 trial credits **plus** your plan's monthly credits (Starter 4,500 in total, Pro 15,500, Agency 80,500) | Your plan's monthly limit | Your plan's monthly limit |
| Cancelled, paused, or the card failed | 500 credits | 1 article | 10 searches |

<Note>
  If you cancel during the trial, you keep access until the trial ends. Your workspace becomes read-only as soon as the 500 credits are spent or the trial ends, whichever comes first.
</Note>

The first check of up to 5 tracked prompts is free during the trial. When the trial turns into a paid plan, the credits you used during the trial are counted toward your first paid month (minus the 500 free trial credits), so your first month isn't doubled.

## How credits work

Every organization has **one credit pool** shared across all its workspaces. Paid actions draw from it, and it resets every month on your billing date (also monthly on yearly plans).

Some actions also have their own monthly limit on top of the credit pool:

- **Articles:** 8, 30 or 120 a month, depending on your plan.
- **Keyword research searches:** 150, 400 or 1,500 a month.

When you reach either limit, Spyro AI tells you the date it resets.

### What each action costs

| Action | Credits | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Write an article | **250** | Research, writing and images included |
| Check one prompt on one AI engine | **10** | **20** on Claude. A prompt checked on 5 engines costs 60 per check |
| Spyro AI answer | **10** | Each AI agent reply, **Ask Spyro AI** answer or AI Helper answer in the writer |
| Keyword research search | **10** | Also counts toward your monthly search limit |
| Keyword analysis | **20** | Opening the detailed view of one keyword |
| Competitor research | **100** per competitor | Free for a competitor already scanned in the last 30 days, unless you choose to refresh it |
| Rank check | **2** per keyword, per check | Weekly checks are about 9 credits a month per keyword; daily checks about 60 |
| Additional workspace | **300** | Your first workspace is free |
| Site audits, crawls and re-checks | **Free** | Including the automatic weekly audit |

<Tip>
  When you add a tracked prompt, Spyro AI shows an estimate of its monthly credit cost before you save it.
</Tip>

## Where to see your credits

- **Sidebar:** the bottom of the sidebar shows your plan and the credits used out of your monthly limit, with the reset date. During the trial it shows the trial allowance and the days left.
- **Usage:** open **Settings → Usage** in a workspace to see what each action cost in that workspace this cycle. The organization's **Usage** page shows the total for all workspaces.
- **Plan & billing:** shows your current plan, the credits used this cycle and when credits reset.

## Running out of credits

If an action would cost more credits than you have left, Spyro AI blocks it and tells you how many credits it needs and how many you have. You can wait for your monthly reset or move to a larger plan.

## Change your plan or manage billing

Open your profile menu (your avatar, top right) and choose **Billing**. This opens your organization's **Plan & billing** page.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a plan">
    Choose Starter, Pro or Agency, monthly or yearly, then confirm the switch.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for your next billing date">
    Plan changes take effect on your **next billing date**, with no charge today. Until then you keep your current plan. You can cancel a scheduled change with **Cancel scheduled change**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manage your billing">
    Use **Manage billing** to update your card, download invoices and review your subscription.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Billing covers your whole organization, not a single workspace. Owners and admins can open **Plan & billing** and **Manage billing**. Only the organization **owner** can switch plans. Other team members see a message that only owners and admins can manage billing.
</Note>
