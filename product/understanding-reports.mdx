After you [run an audit](/product/audit), Spyro AI shows you a report. It looks like a
lot at first, but it's built around two scores and a to-do list. This page explains
what each part means and what to do about it.

## Your two scores

Spyro AI grades your website out of 100 in two areas. The same scores have slightly different names on different screens:

| Score | On the audit page | On the dashboard | In plain words |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| How well your site is set up to rank on Google | **Site Health** | **SEO health** | "Can Google find, understand and rank my pages?" |
| How well your site is set up to be read and cited by AI engines | **AI Search** | **GEO readiness** | "Can ChatGPT and other AI engines understand and quote me?" |

### What goes into them

Both scores come from the checks Spyro AI runs on every page it reads, plus a few checks of your site as a whole. The **Overview** tab shows your health in six categories:

- **Technical SEO**: titles, meta descriptions, headings, broken links and redirects, structured data, indexing and image alt text.
- **Advanced SEO**: language tags, social sharing tags, orphan and thin pages, your sitemap and your `robots.txt` rules.
- **Performance**: Core Web Vitals and slow pages.
- **Mobile**: whether your pages are set up for phone screens.
- **Security**: HTTPS and mixed content.
- **AI Visibility**: whether AI crawlers can read your site and how easy your content is for AI engines to quote.

Every issue Spyro AI finds lowers the score of the page it's on. Fixing issues, starting with errors, raises it.

### What the numbers mean

- **85-100: Excellent.** You're in great shape.
- **70-84: Good.** Solid, with a few things to tidy up.
- **50-69: Needs work.** Worth spending some time here.
- **Below 50: Poor.** Start fixing the biggest issues first.

<Tip>
Don't aim for a perfect 100. Fixing the **errors** first usually gives you
the biggest jump for the least effort.
</Tip>

## Your list of fixes

The **Issues & fixes** tab lists every issue Spyro AI found, grouped by how much it matters:

| Severity | Counted as | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Critical** | Errors | Fix these first. They can stop you from ranking or being read. |
| **Warning** | Warnings | Worth fixing soon. |
| **Notice** | Notices | Small improvements and nice-to-haves. |

Every issue tells you **what's wrong** and **how to fix it** in plain language. For critical issues and warnings you can also choose **Suggest a fix** for an AI-written recommendation tailored to your site, which you can follow yourself or hand to whoever manages your site.

## Looking at individual pages

The **Pages** tab gives each page its own SEO and GEO scores and a health badge (**Critical**, **Warning**, **Notice** or **Healthy**, from its most serious issue). Choose any page to open its report, with its issues and a history of its scores.

The **History** tab shows your scores over time, the change since your previous audit, your best score so far and any newly detected issues.

## Can AI engines read your site?

Your report checks whether AI crawlers are **allowed** to read your website: GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, CCBot, Bytespider, Amazonbot and Applebot-Extended. Some sites block them by accident. If your `robots.txt` blocks any of them from your whole site, Spyro AI flags it as a **critical** issue, because if AI engines can't read your site, they can't recommend you.

## Download or share your report

Choose **Export report to PDF** on the audit page to get a clean copy of the full report. It's handy for sharing with clients, teammates or your web developer.

## Ask about your report

Not sure what an issue means? Choose **Ask Spyro AI** on the audit page to ask questions about your report in plain English, like _"What should I fix first?"_ Each answer costs 10 credits.

## What to expect

- Scores update after every audit, including the automatic weekly one.
- Bigger sites take a little longer to check.
- Improving your scores takes a few rounds: fix issues, re-run the audit, and watch the numbers climb.
