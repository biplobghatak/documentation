Competitor Research lets you compare your website against up to 5 competitors in a single scan. Spyro pulls keyword ranking data for each domain, builds a side-by-side comparison, and shows you exactly which keywords your competitors rank for that you don't - plus where you share ground and where you have quick wins. You can run additional scans later with another set of up to 5 competitor domains.

## Where to find it

Open **Competitor research** from the sidebar inside any workspace. The dashboard has four tabs: **Overview**, **Content gaps**, **Competitors**, and **AI Analysis**.

## Running a scan

### What you enter

When you start a new scan, you'll set up the following:

- **Your domain** — pulled automatically from your workspace settings. This is excluded from the competitor list.
- **Competitor domains** — enter **1 to 5** domains you want to compare against.
- **Location** — the country or region to check rankings in.
- **Language** — the language to check rankings in.
- **Competitor ranking position** — how deep into search results to look: top 10, 20, 50, or 100 positions.
- **Minimum search volume** — optionally filter out very low-volume keywords.
- **Maximum keyword difficulty** — optionally cap results by difficulty score (1-100).
- **Include subdomains** — choose whether to include subdomains in the analysis.

<Note>
  Your own domain is automatically excluded from the competitor list. You cannot add it as a competitor.
</Note>

### Credit cost

Each competitor domain costs **150 credits**. For example, scanning 3 competitors uses 450 credits.

Spyro checks that you have enough credits before the scan starts. If you don't have enough, the scan is blocked and you'll see a message explaining how many credits you need and how many you have left.

Credits are charged only after a scan completes successfully. If a scan fails, you are not charged.

<Note>
  See [Plans & billing](/product/billing-and-usage) for the full list of credit costs and your plan's monthly credit pool.
</Note>

### What happens while the scan runs

The scan runs in the background. You can close the page and come back later - your results will appear when the scan finishes. When the scan finishes, the **Overview** tab loads automatically.

## Overview results

The Overview tab shows a high-level comparison of your domain versus each competitor:

- **Domain rank** — how each domain compares overall.
- **Organic keywords** — total keywords each domain ranks for.
- **Top-10 keywords** — how many keywords each domain ranks in the top 10.
- **Estimated traffic/month** — estimated monthly organic traffic for each domain.
- **AI verdict** — an AI-generated summary of the competitive landscape.
- **Topic-gap cluster cards** — highlight areas where competitors outrank you, linking directly into the Content gaps view.

## Content gaps

Click the **Content gaps** tab to see the full keyword-level comparison. This is where you'll spend most of your time planning content.

### Gaps, Shared, and Quick wins

Results are split into three bucket tabs:

- **Gaps** — keywords at least one competitor ranks for that you don't rank for at all.
- **Shared** — keywords both you and your competitors rank for.
- **Quick wins** — gap keywords where at least one competitor ranks in the top 10 and you rank lower or not at all. These are your highest-priority opportunities.

### Topic clusters

Spyro uses AI to group gap keywords into topic clusters, helping you plan content around themes rather than individual keywords. Clusters only form when there are at least 8 gap keywords, and up to 6 clusters are shown. Click a cluster chip to filter the keyword table to that topic.

### Keyword table

The keyword table shows detailed data for every keyword in the scan:

| Column | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Keyword | The search term |
| Volume | Monthly search volume |
| CPC | Cost per click (indicates commercial intent) |
| Difficulty | How hard it is to rank for this keyword |
| Intent | What the searcher is looking for (informational, transactional, etc.) |
| Your rank | Where you rank, or blank if you don't rank |
| Competitor ranks | Where each competitor ranks |
| Best competitor position | The highest rank any competitor holds |
| Best competitor URL | The competitor page that ranks highest |

### Write article

Click **Write article** on any keyword row to jump straight to the AI writer with that keyword pre-filled. This is the fastest way to turn a gap into content.

### Track keyword

Click **Track keyword** to add any keyword from the table to your [rank tracker](/product/rank-tracker) so you can monitor whether your new content closes the gap.

### Export to CSV

Click **Export to CSV** to download the full keyword table for offline analysis, reporting, or sharing with your team.

## Competitors tab

The **Competitors** tab shows profile cards for each competitor in the scan, including their headline metrics and how they compare to you at a glance.

## AI Analysis tab

The **AI Analysis** tab provides an AI-generated synthesis of the scan results, summarizing the most important findings and strategic takeaways from the competitive comparison.

## When you don't have enough credits

If your credit balance is too low to cover the scan, Spyro blocks the scan before it starts and shows you:

- How many credits the scan would cost.
- How many credits you have remaining.

You can upgrade your plan or wait for your monthly credit reset. See [Plans & billing](/product/billing-and-usage) for options.

## Error and empty states

- **No scan yet** — when you first open Competitor Research, you'll see an empty state with a prompt to start your first scan.
- **Scan failed** — if a scan fails (for example, due to a temporary data issue), you'll see an error message. You are **not** charged credits for failed scans.

## Limits

| Limit | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Competitors per scan | up to **5** |
| Keyword rows stored per scan | up to **2,000** |
| Topic clusters shown | up to **6** |
