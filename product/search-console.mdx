The **GSC & Bing** page brings your real search data from **Google Search Console** and **Bing Webmaster Tools** into Spyro AI. You see exactly how people find you on Google and Bing: clicks and impressions, the pages and searches that bring visitors, index status, and where there are easy wins.

Both are free tools from Google and Microsoft. Connecting each one takes about a minute, and Spyro AI only **reads** your data. It can't change anything in your Google or Bing account.

## How to connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open GSC & Bing">
    Choose **GSC & Bing** in the sidebar, under **Search (SEO)**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect Google, Bing or both">
    Choose **Connect Google Search Console** or **Connect Bing Webmaster Tools**, and sign in with the account that has access to your website. Approve the request.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your site">
    Spyro AI lists the sites your account can see. Pick the one for this workspace and choose **Use this site**. If a site's domain doesn't match your workspace, it's marked **Different domain**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for your data">
    Spyro AI starts importing your performance data. Your numbers appear once the first import finishes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Google reports search data with a delay of a few days, so the most recent days may look low at first.
</Note>

## What you'll see

Use the **Search engine** switch at the top to view **All engines** (Google and Bing side by side), **GSC** or **Bing**.

### Google Search Console

The Google view has these tabs:

| Tab | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | Total clicks, impressions, average click-through rate and average position, with a trend chart. Filter by search type, device and date range |
| **Pages** | Your pages and how each performs |
| **Queries** | The searches people used to find you |
| **Keywords** | Keyword-level performance |
| **Location** | Performance by country |
| **Search appearance** | How your results appear in Google |
| **Discover & News** | Traffic from Google Discover and Google News |
| **Opportunities** | Suggested fixes, below |
| **Indexing** | Index status per URL and your submitted sitemaps |

The **Opportunities** tab pairs your search data with your latest [site audit](/product/audit):

- **Seen but not clicked:** pages many people see but few click, with the clicks you're missing each month. A better title usually helps (**Rewrite title**).
- **Almost ranking:** searches where you sit just off the top results, with an estimate of the extra clicks a top-5 spot would bring (**Improve page**).
- **Pages losing traffic:** pages whose clicks are dropping and may need a refresh.
- **Technical:** mismatches between what Spyro AI crawled and what Search Console reports, plus sitemap problems.

### Bing Webmaster Tools

The Bing view shows daily clicks and impressions on Bing, your top pages and queries, crawl activity and crawl issues, inbound links, submitted sitemaps, and blocked URLs. Some details are only shared with site owners in Bing.

### All engines

The combined view compares Google and Bing: total clicks, each engine's share of clicks, daily clicks by engine, and the pages and queries that appear on both.

## Get help from Spyro AI

Choose **Fix with Spyro** to ask Spyro AI about your search data, for example which pages to improve first. Each answer costs 10 credits.

## Manage the connection

- **Re-sync** pulls in fresh data.
- **Disconnect GSC** or **Disconnect Bing** removes Spyro AI's access. Your data stays in Google or Bing, and you can reconnect any time.
- If a connection shows **Reconnect needed**, connect again to restore access.

## Tips

- Connect early: the sooner your accounts are linked, the more history Spyro AI can show you.
- Choose the site that exactly matches your workspace, so the suggestions line up with your pages.
- After you fix something, check back here after a few days to see the effect.
