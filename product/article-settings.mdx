Content settings tell Spyro AI how you want your articles to sound, look and get published. Set them once and every new article follows them. You'll find them in the sidebar under **Create → Article Planner**, as four pages: **Blog & Content**, **Article Defaults**, **Image Generation** and **CMS Integration**.

## Why use it

- **Stay on-brand.** Every article follows your style, your colors and a consistent image look.
- **Set it and forget it.** Choose your preferences once and they apply to all future articles.
- **Publish automatically.** Connect your website and let Spyro AI post for you.

## Blog & Content

Point Spyro AI at your existing content so it can learn your voice:

- **Sitemap URL**, for example `https://example.com/sitemap.xml`.
- **Main blog path** (optional), where your articles live, for example `/blog`.
- **Best article examples**: a few of your best articles for Spyro AI to learn from.

## Article Defaults

How every article is written and published by default:

- **Article style**: **Informational**, **Commercial** or **Mixed**.
- **Global instructions**: instructions applied to every article, for example _"Always include a practical example."_
- **GEO Mode**: when on, articles are written answer-first and stat-dense, with an FAQ section and schema, to help them get cited by ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude and Google AI Overviews. When off, articles use standard SEO writing.
- **Auto-publish**: when on (the default), finished articles are sent to your connected website automatically.
- **Publish as draft**: when on, new articles are saved as drafts in your CMS (for example Shopify or WordPress) so you can review them before publishing. When off, they go live immediately.

<Tip>
  You can still adjust a single article in the calendar or the writer; these are only the defaults.
</Tip>

## Image Generation

Choose whether and how Spyro AI creates images:

- **Featured image** and **In-content images**: turn each on or off.
- **Image style**: pick a preset (**Illustration**, **Realistic**, **Sketch**, **Watercolor** or **Brand & Text**) or describe your own with **Custom**. Your brand colors are applied to every image.
- **Featured image format**: **Auto**, or one of several title-card layouts.
- **Featured image size**: for example a 1280 × 720 wide banner, 1200 × 800 standard or 1080 × 1080 square, or a custom size.

## Brand voice and colors

Spyro AI learns your **tone profile** and **brand profile** (brand colors and style) from your website. You'll find both in **Settings → General**, where you can refresh them after your site changes.

## CMS Integration

Connect the website Spyro AI publishes to. Today you can connect:

- **WordPress.org**: your self-hosted WordPress site, through the Spyro Autopilot plugin.
- **Shopify**: publish to your Shopify blog.
- **Webhooks**: send each article to another system.

Wix, Framer and Webflow are shown as **Coming soon** and can't be connected yet. For setup steps, see [Integrations](/product/integrations).

## What to expect

Once your style, images and integration are set, you don't have to think about them again: every new article follows them. Each integration shows whether it's connected and a history of recent deliveries, so you can see at a glance that everything works.

## Tips

- Set your article style and image style **before** you write your first article, so it comes out right the first time.
- Refresh your tone profile whenever your website changes meaningfully.
- Turn on **Publish as draft** until you're happy with how articles look on your site, so you can review each one before it goes live.
