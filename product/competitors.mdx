Spyro AI tracks your competitors in three places. Each answers a different question, so this page tells you which one to open.

| You want to know | Open | What you get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who my competitors are | **Settings → Competitors** | The list of competitors Spyro AI tracks for this website |
| Which keywords and topics they win that I don't | **Competitor research** | A keyword gap scan of up to 5 competitors |
| Which brands AI engines recommend | **Dashboard** and **AI visibility** | How often each brand is named in AI answers to your tracked prompts |

## Your competitor list

Open **Settings → Competitors**. You'll see the competitors Spyro AI found when you set up your workspace, plus any you added. Spyro AI discovers more automatically as you research keywords.

- To add one, enter its website (for example `competitor.com`).
- To remove one, delete it from the list.

These competitors appear as suggestions when you set up a [competitor research](/product/competitor-research) scan.

## Keywords and topics they win

[Competitor research](/product/competitor-research) compares your site with up to 5 competitors. It shows the keywords they rank for and you don't, quick wins, the topics you're missing and the competitor pages you could outrank. Each competitor costs 100 credits per scan, and a competitor scanned in the last 30 days is free to include again.

## Which brands AI engines recommend

When you track prompts, Spyro AI records every brand the AI engines name in their answers.

- The dashboard's **Brands** card ranks the brands with the highest AI visibility, with your own brand marked **You**.
- [AI visibility](/product/ai-visibility) shows how your visibility and theirs change over time.
- Each [prompt](/product/ai-visibility/prompts) shows which competitor sites are cited for that question.

## How to use them together

<Steps>
  <Step title="Build your competitor list">
    Open **Settings → Competitors** and keep the rivals that really matter.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find their keyword gaps">
    Run a [competitor research](/product/competitor-research) scan and start with **Quick wins**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check who AI recommends">
    Look at the **Brands** card on your dashboard and at [AI visibility](/product/ai-visibility).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn gaps into content">
    Choose **Write article** on a gap keyword to add it to your [content calendar](/product/calendar).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Start with 3 to 5 close competitors so the comparisons stay meaningful.
</Tip>
