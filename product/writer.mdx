The **AI writer** writes a full article for you. You choose a topic, and Spyro AI
researches it, writes the draft and adds images, so you start with a finished
piece instead of a blank page. From there you can polish it, check how well it's
optimized, and publish it to your website or export it.

## Why use it

- **Go from idea to article in minutes.** Spyro AI does the research and writing for
  you.
- **Images included.** A featured image and in-article images are created
  automatically, in the style you chose.
- **Built for search and AI.** Every draft is written to rank, and you get an article
  score so you know where it stands.

## How to use it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a topic">
    Choose **Generate Article** on an idea in your [content calendar](/product/calendar), or ask the [AI agent](/product/spyro-ai-agent) to send an idea to the writer. Keywords you pick with **Write article** in [competitor research](/product/competitor-research) or keyword research are added to your calendar first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let Spyro AI write it">
    Spyro AI researches the topic (web research, keyword data and your own site), writes the sections, runs quality checks and adds the featured image. You can follow its progress, and the editor opens when the article is ready.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit and polish">
    Review the draft in the editor. Change the wording, headings and formatting, add tables and images, and fine-tune the title, slug and meta description. Undo and redo work as usual.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the article score">
    The score panel shows your **Article score**, broken down into title, on-page SEO, readability and keyword use, with your target keyword's search volume and difficulty. Use it to make quick improvements.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish or export">
    Open the **Publish and export** menu. Under **Publish to**, pick a connected website. Under **Export**, choose **Copy markdown**, **Download .md**, **Download .docx** or **Export HTML**. You can also schedule an article to publish at a set date and time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Improve it with the AI Helper

The **AI Helper** is a chat panel inside the writer. Describe a change, or pick a suggestion such as _"Strengthen the intro hook and tone"_ or _"Add a FAQ section based on SERP questions"_. It proposes the edit in the article for you to accept (Enter) or reject (Esc). Turn on web search when you want it to look up fresh facts. Each answer costs **10 credits**.

## What it costs

Writing an article costs **250 credits** and counts toward your plan's monthly article limit (8 on Starter, 30 on Pro, 120 on Agency). Editing, scoring and exporting are free. See [Plans & billing](/product/billing-and-usage).

## What to expect

Writing usually takes a short while; the editor opens on its own when everything is done. If something gets stuck, choose **Retry Generation** to start it again.

## Tips

- Set your article style, GEO mode and image style in
  [Content settings](/product/article-settings) before you write, so every article
  comes out on-brand.
- Connect your website first in [Integrations](/product/integrations) so you can
  publish without leaving the page.
- Use the article score as a guide: small edits to your title, meta description and
  headings often give it a quick boost.
