Short answers to the questions people ask most. If you don't see yours here, reach
out to support and we'll help.

## Getting started

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to be technical to use Spyro AI?">
    No. Spyro AI is built for marketers, writers, and business owners. If you can use a
    website, you can use Spyro AI. The only slightly technical step is connecting your
    blog to publish articles, and we walk you through it in
    [Integrations](/product/integrations).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is there a free trial?">
    Yes. New accounts get a **3-day free trial**. You choose a plan and enter your card at
    checkout, but you aren't charged until the trial ends, and you can cancel before then.
    While the trial is active you get 500 trial credits plus your plan's monthly credits.
    See [Plans & billing](/product/billing-and-usage) for the full trial rules.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens if I cancel during the trial?">
    You keep access until the trial ends, with 500 credits, 1 article and 10 keyword
    research searches. Your workspace becomes read-only as soon as the 500 credits are
    spent or the trial ends, whichever comes first.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I add my website?">
    You add it during setup, right after you choose your plan. Spyro AI takes a quick
    look at your site to learn your brand, audience, and competitors. See
    [Set up your account](/product/onboarding).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I track more than one website?">
    Yes. Each website lives in its own **workspace**. Starter supports up to 2
    workspaces, Pro up to 10, and Agency unlimited. Your first workspace is free; each
    additional one costs 300 credits. See [Plans & billing](/product/billing-and-usage).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Plans and pricing

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How much does Spyro AI cost?">
    Starter is \$29/month (or \$288/year) for up to 2 workspaces and 4,000 credits/month.
    Pro is \$99/month (or \$990/year) for up to 10 workspaces and 15,000 credits/month.
    Agency is \$399/month (or \$3,990/year) for unlimited workspaces and 80,000 credits/month.
    Yearly billing costs about the same as 10 months of monthly billing. Full details on
    [Plans & billing](/product/billing-and-usage).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How many articles can I write each month?">
    Each plan has a monthly article limit: **8** on Starter, **30** on Pro and **120** on
    Agency. Each article also costs **250 credits** from your credit pool, so you need both
    an article left in your limit and enough credits. A cancelled trial allows 1 article.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How many keywords can I track?">
    You can track up to **100 keywords** per workspace on Starter, **500** on Pro,
    and **1,500** on Agency in the [rank tracker](/product/rank-tracker). Deleting a
    keyword frees its slot right away.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I add my team?">
    Yes. Team members are unlimited on all plans. Invite teammates and choose what they
    can access. See [Team members](/product/team-members).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What are credits?">
    Credits are the currency Spyro AI uses for paid actions like writing articles, checking
    AI answers, running competitor research and viewing keyword analysis. Every organization
    has one credit pool shared across all its workspaces: 4,000 credits/month on Starter,
    15,000 on Pro and 80,000 on Agency.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What uses credits?">
    Writing an article (250 credits), checking a prompt on one AI engine (10 credits, or 20
    on Claude), each Spyro AI answer (10), each keyword research search (10), each keyword
    analysis (20), competitor research (100 per competitor, free if that competitor was
    scanned in the last 30 days), rank checks (2 per keyword per check), and each additional
    workspace (300). Site audits and crawls are free. See
    [Plans & billing](/product/billing-and-usage) for the full table.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="When do my credits reset?">
    Every month on your billing date, also on yearly plans. Your monthly article and
    keyword research limits reset at the same time.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens when I run out of credits?">
    Paid actions are blocked until your credits reset or you move to a larger plan.
    There is no self-serve credit top-up. For higher volumes, [contact us](https://www.spyro.app/contact)
    about a custom plan.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Features

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Which AI assistants does Spyro AI track?">
    ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Gemini, Claude and Perplexity. Starter tracks ChatGPT
    and Google AI Overviews; Pro and Agency track all 5. See
    [AI visibility](/product/ai-visibility).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How does Spyro AI know if AI assistants mention me?">
    It asks them the kinds of questions your buyers ask, then reads the answers to see
    whether your brand (or a competitor) is named. Because AI answers change each time,
    results are shown as a rate across many checks rather than a single yes or no.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can Spyro AI publish to my website?">
    Yes. You can connect **WordPress** (self-hosted, with the Spyro Autopilot plugin),
    **Shopify**, or a **webhook**, then publish articles with one click or automatically.
    See [Integrations](/product/integrations).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Spyro AI write the whole article for me?">
    Yes. The [AI writer](/product/writer) researches your topic, writes a full
    SEO-ready draft, and adds images. You can edit anything before you publish.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I connect Google Search Console and Bing?">
    Yes. Connect **Google Search Console** and **Bing Webmaster Tools** on the
    **GSC & Bing** page to see your real clicks, impressions and positions. See
    [GSC & Bing](/product/search-console).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## What to expect

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How long does an audit take?">
    It depends on the size of your site: Spyro AI checks up to 150, 1,000 or 1,500 pages
    depending on your plan. The audit page updates by itself while it runs, so you can
    leave and come back.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How often does Spyro AI check my site, rankings and AI answers?">
    Your **site audit** runs automatically once a week. Each **tracked prompt** and each
    **tracked keyword** is checked weekly or daily, depending on the schedule you chose for
    it. New keywords and new prompts are checked right away when you add them.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My scores aren't perfect - is that bad?">
    Not at all. Fix the **errors** first, re-run your audit, and watch your scores climb
    over time. See [Understand your reports](/product/understanding-reports).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I change or cancel my plan?">
    Open your profile menu (top right), choose **Billing**, and pick a new plan. Plan
    changes take effect on your next billing date. To update your card, see invoices or
    cancel, use **Manage billing** on the same page. See
    [Plans & billing](/product/billing-and-usage).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I change my password?">
    If you signed up with an email and password, use **Forgot password?** on the login
    page to set a new one. If you sign in with Google, manage your password in your
    Google account.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
