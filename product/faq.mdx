Short answers to the questions people ask most. If you don't see yours here, reach
out to support and we'll help.

## Getting started

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to be technical to use Spyro AI?">
    No. Spyro AI is built for marketers, writers, and business owners. If you can use a
    website, you can use Spyro AI. The only slightly technical step is connecting your
    blog to publish articles, and we walk you through it in
    [Integrations](/product/integrations).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is there a free trial?">
    Yes. New accounts get a **3-day free trial** that includes **500 credits** and
    **1 article** generation, so you can try the writer and other paid features before you pay.
    Unused trial credits are cleared when you subscribe.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I add my website?">
    You add it when you sign up. Spyro AI takes a quick look at your site to learn your
    brand, audience, and competitors. See [Set up your account](/product/onboarding).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I track more than one website?">
    Yes. Each website lives in its own **workspace**. The **Starter** plan supports up to
    2 workspaces; the **Pro** plan supports up to 10 workspaces; and the **Agency**
    plan supports unlimited workspaces. See [Plans & billing](/product/billing-and-usage).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Plans and pricing

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How much does Spyro AI cost?">
    **Starter** is \$29/month (or \$288/year) for up to 2 workspaces and 4,000 credits/month.
    **Pro** is \$99/month (or \$990/year) for up to 10 workspaces and 15,000 credits/month.
    **Agency** is \$399/month (or \$3,990/year) for unlimited workspaces and 80,000 credits/month.
    Paying yearly saves up to 20% (two months free). You can also use code **BETA50** at checkout
    for 50% off your first month on monthly billing. Full details on [Plans & billing](/product/billing-and-usage).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How many articles can I write each month?">
    Writing an article costs **250 credits**. Starter includes 4,000 credits/month (up to 8 articles/month),
    Pro includes 15,000 credits/month (up to 30 articles/month), and Agency includes 80,000 credits/month
    (up to 120 articles/month), so the number of articles you can write depends on your plan and what else
    you use credits for. The free trial includes 1 article total. See [Plans & billing](/product/billing-and-usage).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How many keywords can I track?">
    You can track up to **100 keywords** per brand on Starter, up to **500 keywords** on Pro,
    and up to **1,500 keywords** on Agency in the [rank tracker](/product/rank-tracker).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I add my team?">
    Yes. Invite teammates and choose what they can access. Team members are unlimited across all plans.
    See [Team members](/product/team-members).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What are credits?">
    Credits are the currency Spyro AI uses for paid actions like writing articles, checking AI citations,
    running competitor scans, and viewing keyword analysis. Every organisation has one credit pool shared
    across all workspaces. Starter includes 4,000 credits/month; Pro includes 15,000 credits/month; Agency
    includes 80,000 credits/month.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What uses credits?">
    Writing an article (250 credits), checking AI citations (10 credits per prompt × engine, Claude 20 credits),
    Spyro AI answers (10 credits), keyword research searches (10 credits), keyword analysis (20 credits),
    running competitor research scans (150 credits per competitor), LLM mentions scans (70 credits per tracked competitor, monthly),
    rank checks (2 credits per keyword, weekly), and creating additional workspaces (300 credits, first workspace free).
    Site audits, SERP lookups, and site-intel snapshots are completely free. See [Plans & billing](/product/billing-and-usage) for the full list of credit costs.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="When do my credits reset?">
    Credits reset monthly on your billing date.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens when I run out of credits?">
    Paid actions are blocked until you upgrade your plan or your credits reset next
    month. There is no self-serve credit top-up; for higher volumes, choose a higher
    plan or contact sales for a Custom plan.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Features

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Which AI assistants does Spyro AI track?">
    ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, and Google's AI Overviews. Spyro AI checks
    whether they mention your brand. Starter tracks 2 engines (ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews); Pro and Agency track all 5 engines. See [AI visibility & competitors](/product/ai-visibility).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How does Spyro AI know if AI assistants mention me?">
    It asks them the kinds of questions your buyers ask, then reads the answers to
    see if you (or your competitors) are mentioned. Because AI answers change each
    time, Spyro AI checks more than once and shows the result as "mentioned in X of Y
    checks" instead of a simple yes or no.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can Spyro AI publish to my website?">
    Yes. You can connect **WordPress (self-hosted)**, **Shopify**, or a
    **webhook**, then publish articles with one click. See
    [Integrations](/product/integrations).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Spyro AI write the whole article for me?">
    Yes. The [AI writer](/product/writer) researches your topic, writes a full
    SEO-ready draft, and adds images. You can edit anything before you publish.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I connect Google Analytics?">
    Yes. You can connect **Google Analytics 4** to track AI-driven referral
    traffic - visitors who reached your site from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini,
    Claude, and other AI engines. See [Integrations](/product/integrations).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## What to expect

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How long does an audit take?">
    Usually a minute or two. Larger websites take a little longer because Spyro AI looks
    at more pages.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How often does Spyro AI check my rankings and AI mentions?">
    Spyro AI checks your **AI visibility** (mentions) **every week** and your
    **keyword rankings** every week. It also checks right away when you add something
    new (like a new keyword or a freshly published article), and you can run checks
    on demand.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My scores aren't perfect - is that bad?">
    Not at all. Focus on the **critical** fixes first, re-run your audit, and watch
    your scores climb over time. See [Understand your reports](/product/understanding-reports).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I change or cancel my plan?">
    Open **Settings** and go to billing. You can upgrade, downgrade, or manage your
    subscription there. See [Plans & billing](/product/billing-and-usage).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
