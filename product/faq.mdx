Short answers to the questions people ask most. If you don't see yours here, reach
out to support and we'll help.

## Getting started

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to be technical to use Spyro?">
    No. Spyro is built for marketers, writers, and business owners. If you can use a
    website, you can use Spyro. The only slightly technical step is connecting your
    blog to publish articles, and we walk you through it in
    [Integrations](/product/integrations).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is there a free trial?">
    Yes. New accounts get a **3-day free trial** that includes **500 credits** and
    **3 articles**, so you can try the writer and other paid features before you pay.
    Unused trial credits are cleared when you subscribe.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I add my website?">
    You add it when you sign up. Spyro takes a quick look at your site to learn your
    brand, audience, and competitors. See [Set up your account](/product/onboarding).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I track more than one website?">
    Yes. Each website lives in its own **workspace**. The **Pro** plan supports up to
    10 workspaces; the **Agency** plan supports unlimited workspaces. See
    [Plans & billing](/product/billing-and-usage).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Plans and pricing

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How much does Spyro cost?">
    **Pro** is \$99/month (or \$990/year) for up to 10 workspaces and 15,000
    credits/month. **Agency** is \$399/month (or \$3,990/year) for unlimited
    workspaces and 80,000 credits/month. Paying yearly saves you two months. Full
    details on [Plans & billing](/product/billing-and-usage).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How many articles can I write each month?">
    Writing an article costs **150 credits**. Pro includes 15,000 credits/month and
    Agency includes 80,000 credits/month, so the number of articles you can write
    depends on your plan and what else you use credits for. The free trial includes
    3 articles total. See [Plans & billing](/product/billing-and-usage).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How many keywords can I track?">
    You can track up to **500 keywords** at once per website in the
    [rank tracker](/product/rank-tracker).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I add my team?">
    Yes. Invite teammates and choose what they can access. See
    [Team members](/product/team-members).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What are credits?">
    Credits are the currency Spyro uses for paid actions like writing articles,
    running competitor scans, and viewing keyword analysis. Every organisation has
    one credit pool shared across all workspaces. Pro includes 15,000 credits/month;
    Agency includes 80,000 credits/month.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What uses credits?">
    Writing an article (150), running a competitor research scan (150 per competitor), viewing keyword analysis (20 credits for the first view of a keyword in a specific location and language), keyword research searches (10), AI agent answers (5), rank checks (2), SERP lookups (13), site intel runs (30, weekly), LLM mentions scans (70 per tracked competitor, monthly), and creating additional workspaces (300, first workspace free). See [Plans & billing](/product/billing-and-usage) for the full list of credit costs.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="When do my credits reset?">
    Credits reset monthly on your billing date.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens when I run out of credits?">
    Paid actions are blocked until you upgrade your plan or your credits reset next
    month. There is no self-serve credit top-up; for higher volumes, choose a higher
    plan or contact sales for a Custom plan.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Features

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Which AI assistants does Spyro track?">
    ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, and Google's AI Overviews. Spyro checks
    whether they mention your brand. See [AI visibility & competitors](/product/ai-visibility).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How does Spyro know if AI assistants mention me?">
    It asks them the kinds of questions your buyers ask, then reads the answers to
    see if you (or your competitors) are mentioned. Because AI answers change each
    time, Spyro checks more than once and shows the result as "mentioned in X of Y
    checks" instead of a simple yes or no.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can Spyro publish to my website?">
    Yes. You can connect **WordPress (self-hosted)**, **Shopify**, or a
    **webhook**, then publish articles with one click. See
    [Integrations](/product/integrations).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Spyro write the whole article for me?">
    Yes. The [AI writer](/product/writer) researches your topic, writes a full
    SEO-ready draft, and adds images. You can edit anything before you publish.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I connect Google Analytics?">
    Yes. You can connect **Google Analytics 4** to track AI-driven referral
    traffic - visitors who reached your site from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini,
    Claude, and other AI engines. See [Integrations](/product/integrations).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## What to expect

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How long does an audit take?">
    Usually a minute or two. Larger websites take a little longer because Spyro looks
    at more pages.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How often does Spyro check my rankings and AI mentions?">
    Spyro checks your **AI visibility** (mentions) **every week** and your
    **keyword rankings** every week. It also checks right away when you add something
    new (like a new keyword or a freshly published article), and you can run checks
    on demand.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My scores aren't perfect - is that bad?">
    Not at all. Focus on the **critical** fixes first, re-run your audit, and watch
    your scores climb over time. See [Understand your reports](/product/understanding-reports).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I change or cancel my plan?">
    Open **Settings** and go to billing. You can upgrade, downgrade, or manage your
    subscription there. See [Plans & billing](/product/billing-and-usage).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
