An **audit** is a full health check of your website. Spyro AI reads your pages and scores your site on two things: classic **SEO** (how well Google can find, read and rank your pages) and **AI readiness** (how easily AI engines like ChatGPT can understand and quote your content). Every issue is grouped by how serious it is, so you know what to fix first.

Audits are **free**. They don't use any credits.

## Why it matters

You can't improve what you can't see. An audit turns your whole site into a to-do list: a clear score, the problems dragging it down, and how to fix each one. Run one now to get your baseline, then watch your score climb as you fix things.

## How to run an audit

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the audit">
    Choose **SEO + GEO audit** in the sidebar, under **Optimize**. The page is called **Site audit**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the check">
    Choose **Run audit**. The button shows **Crawling…** while Spyro AI reads your site, and the page updates itself, so you don't need to reload or wait around.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read your report">
    When it's done, your scores and issues appear. Choose **Export report to PDF** to download a copy.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Automatic weekly audits

After your first audit, Spyro AI re-audits your site **once a week** on its own, early in the morning (your workspace's time zone), on a fixed day between Monday and Thursday. You can also run an audit yourself whenever you like.

### How many pages are checked

| Plan | Pages per audit |
| --- | --- |
| Starter | Up to 150 |
| Pro | Up to 1,000 |
| Agency | Up to 1,500 |

If Spyro AI misses a page, add it yourself in **Settings → Knowledge base**.

## What you'll see

At the top of the report are two score rings, each out of 100:

- **Site Health:** your SEO score. The dashboard calls this **SEO health**.
- **AI Search:** your AI-readiness score. The dashboard calls this **GEO readiness**.

Next to them are the number of **Errors**, **Warnings** and **Notices** found across your site.

The report has five tabs:

| Tab | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | Your scores, health by category, and how your score improves as you work through the fix list |
| **Pages** | Every page checked, with its SEO and GEO scores and a health badge. Search, filter by severity and sort, for example **Lowest score first**. Choose a page to open its full report |
| **Issues & fixes** | Every issue found, grouped by severity, with **How to fix it** for each |
| **History** | Your scores over time, the change since the previous audit, and newly detected issues |
| **URL Checks** | Pages you want to check on demand and keep an eye on |

To learn how to read your scores and issues, see [Understand your reports](/product/understanding-reports).

## Fix issues with Spyro AI's help

- **Suggest a fix:** on any critical issue or warning, choose **Suggest a fix** for a short recommendation tailored to your site. It's free. It's advice for you to apply: Spyro AI doesn't change your site.
- **Track your fixes:** mark an issue as fixed once you've dealt with it; it then shows **Marked as fixed**. Choose **Unmark** to undo.
- **Ask Spyro AI:** chat with a helper that knows your audit. Ask things like _"Why is my AI score low?"_ or _"What should I fix first?"_ Each answer costs 10 credits.

## Check one page again

You don't need to re-audit your whole site to test a fix:

- **Re-check after fixes:** open a page's report and choose this to re-check just that page. Its updated findings appear when the check finishes, and the page's **History** shows how it changed.
- **URL Checks:** add an important page on the **URL Checks** tab to check its SEO and GEO scores on demand. Choose **Re-check** any time, or **Remove** it when you no longer need it.

## Tips

- Run your first audit right after setup to get your starting scores.
- Fix the **Errors** first, since they have the biggest impact, then work through Warnings and Notices.
- Check the **History** tab after each weekly audit to see your progress.
